Materials Calculator

An iPhone app to estimate how much paint, tile or boxed flooring to buy. Works offline, no account needed.

FAQ

How do I restore Pro? Settings → Restore purchase. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID.

How do I move my data to another iPhone? Settings → Export backup, then Settings → Import backup on the other iPhone.

Why does the consolidated list buy less? It adds up the needs of compatible materials unrounded and rounds once per group.
